Privacy Policy

Updated November 12, 2026

This policy covers bigsplash.church and the forms inside our Facebook and Instagram ads. It also covers the forms your church fills out after it signs up with Big Splash, plus the emails and texts we send you. Our free Front Door Audit has its own privacy policy.

In short

  • We use your information to onboard your church and run its campaign.
  • Fill out a form in one of our Facebook or Instagram ads and we'll email you about it. Reply stop to any email and the emails end.
  • This website uses the Meta Pixel to measure our ads. Section 6 explains it and how to opt out.
  • We text you only if you checked a texting box. Reply STOP to any text and the texts end.
  • We never sell your information, and we never share your mobile number or text consent for anyone's marketing.
  • Email chris@bigsplash.agency to see, fix or delete what we hold.

1.About us

Big Splash Media LLC runs Big Splash, a marketing agency for churches at 1119 Ascot St., Georgetown, Texas 78626. Chris List runs it. In this policy, “we” and “Big Splash” mean Big Splash Media LLC.

2.The information we collect

From the church intake form:

  • Your name, email and cell number, and the texting boxes you checked
  • Your church's name, legal name, address, website, denomination and weekly attendance
  • Your lead pastor's name and your role at the church
  • Texting registration details: your church's EIN, business type and address, and the name and title of the person who can sign for it

  • Logos, brand files, brand colors and links to your photos and videos

From the forms inside our Facebook and Instagram ads:

  • Your name and email, and the name of your church
  • The ad and form you used, and the time you sent it

From our other pages and emails:

  • The kickoff booking page: the time you pick, your contact details and any note you add
  • The footage page: the videos you upload, your name and email, and your notes about them
  • Checkout: Stripe collects your card or bank details. We never see the full number.
  • This website: the pages you visit and actions like booking a call, recorded by the Meta Pixel (section 6)

3.Our use of it

We use your church's details to run its plan. That means registering your church's texting number with phone carriers, building your plan-your-visit page, editing your videos, running your Facebook and Instagram campaign and sending your family report.

We use your contact details to send onboarding emails and texts, to remind you about open steps and to reply when you write to us.

If you fill out a form in one of our ads, we use your details to send you information on the service you asked about and to invite you to book a call with Chris. We may follow up by email until you book a call or tell us to stop. Our emails record opens and link clicks, and we use that to time our follow-up.

We use website activity from the Meta Pixel to measure our own ads and to show them to people who visited this site.

4.Texts

Big Splash texts you only if you checked a texting box on the intake form and gave a cell number. Texts come from (254) 600-3466.

  • Onboarding and project updates (the first box): reminders about open onboarding steps, your kickoff call, your launch date and the work in progress.
  • News and offers (the second box): occasional updates and offers from Big Splash.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help. Reply STOP to any text and you will get no more texts from us. The terms of service cover the texting program in full.

We share no mobile information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. The sharing listed in section 5 never includes your text opt-in or consent, and we share those with no third party.

5.Companies that see it

We use these companies to do the work. Each one sees only the part it needs:

  • GoHighLevel stores your contact details and form answers, runs the booking page and sends our emails and texts.
  • Phone carriers receive your church's texting registration details through GoHighLevel. Carriers require them before your church's own number can text families.
  • Wasabi stores the videos you upload.
  • Cloudflare runs the footage upload and our checkout. GitHub hosts this site.

  • Slack and ClickUp hold our team's notes and tasks. Slack also gets an alert with your name and email when you fill out one of our ad forms.
  • Meta runs Facebook and Instagram. The forms inside our ads run on Meta too, and Meta passes what you enter in them to us. We run your church's ads from Big Splash Agency, our own Meta business account, with the access your church gives us to its Facebook page and Instagram account. Your church stays the owner and can remove our access anytime. Meta also receives activity from this website through the Meta Pixel (section 6).

We never sell your information. We may share it if the law requires us to.

6.Cookies, the Meta Pixel and ads

This website uses the Meta Pixel, a piece of code from Meta Platforms, Inc. With it, Meta and other third parties may use cookies and similar storage technologies, such as web beacons, to collect or receive information from this website and elsewhere on the internet. They use that information to measure ads and to show ads, including ours, to people who visited.

The pixel records the pages you view here and actions such as booking a call or paying at checkout, along with technical details like your browser and IP address. It doesn't record what you type into our forms, though Meta's automatic matching may send a scrambled (hashed) copy of an email address or phone number you enter so Meta can match ads to their results. Meta handles what it receives under the Meta Privacy Policy.

To opt out of ads based on your activity:

  • On Facebook and Instagram: change your ad settings at facebook.com/adpreferences.
  • Everywhere else: use the opt-out tools at aboutads.info/choices in the US or youronlinechoices.eu in Europe, or block cookies in your browser. This site works without them.

If you filled out one of our ad forms and didn't sign up, we keep your details so we can follow up. Email us anytime and we'll delete them.
