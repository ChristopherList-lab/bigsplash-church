Do you come film at our church?

On VIP Filmed Christmas, yes. Our crew drives to your church in Texas for a 4-hour filming day and turns it into 6 invitation videos. On the Christmas Campaign, you send your livestream and a few phone clips, and we send you a short shot list.

Is it too late for Christmas Eve ads this year?

Christmas Eve 2026 is Thursday, December 24. A campaign does best with 3 to 4 weeks to run, so booking by Thanksgiving week gives you the full window. Invitations start the day you approve them, so a December start still reaches families before Christmas Eve.

Is the ad budget included?

Yes. The Christmas Campaign includes $500 of Facebook and Instagram ad budget, and VIP includes $1,000. You pay once. There's no contract, and nothing renews.

Do we need our own ad account?

No. We run your invitations from Big Splash's ad account, and they show your church's name and page. You give us partner access to your Facebook and Instagram pages. Your church stays the owner, and you can remove us anytime.

Does this work for small-town churches?

Yes. The invitation goes to families within about a 10-minute drive of your building, in a small town or a big metro. Phone clips work, and raw beats polished for invitations.

Can we target Christians on Facebook?

No. Meta removed ad targeting tied to religion in January 2022. Church invitations reach people by where they live, which suits Christmas Eve: the families near your building are the ones who can come back in January.

We're outside Texas. Can we still do this?

Yes, with the Christmas Campaign. It works for churches anywhere, with footage you send us. VIP Filmed Christmas is for churches in Texas, since our crew drives to you.
