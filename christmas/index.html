Questions pastors ask

Good questions.

Is there a contract or commitment?

No. You pay once for this Christmas, and that's it. No contract, no monthly fees, and nothing renews. Your campaign ends after Christmas Eve, and you keep the videos.

We're outside Texas. Can we still do this?

Yes, with the Christmas Campaign. It works for churches anywhere, with footage you send us. VIP Filmed Christmas is for churches in Texas, since our crew drives to you.

Do we need our own ad account?

No. We run your campaign from Big Splash's ad account, and the invitations show your church's name and page. You give us partner access to your Facebook and Instagram pages, and the ad budget is already in the price.

We don't have good video. Can we still do this?

Yes. Phone clips work, and raw beats polished for invitations. We send a short shot list for your pastor. If you'd rather not film at all and your church is in Texas, pick VIP and we'll come to you.

Do the invitations start right away?

They start the day you approve them. A campaign does best with 3 to 4 weeks to run before Christmas Eve, so the earlier you book, the more families see it.

Can we text families from our church?

Yes. Phone carriers make every church register before texting guests. We file the registration for you after our call. Approval takes 1 to 3 weeks, another reason to start early.

After Christmas, then what?

You keep the videos and the list of families who came. If you want invitations running all year, ask about the monthly plan on your call. There's no obligation either way.
