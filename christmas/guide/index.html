Christmas Eve ads for churches: the 2026 guide.

Christmas Eve falls on a Thursday this year. Families who skip church all year still look for a Christmas Eve service, and plenty of them are waiting for someone to ask. Your church can be the one that asks first.

By Chris List, founder of Big Splash in Georgetown, Texas. Updated November 14, 2026.

The short version

  • Start your invitations 3 to 4 weeks before Christmas Eve. In 2026, that means launching between Thursday, November 26 and Thursday, December 3.
  • Invite families to one service at one time, with a button to plan their visit.
  • Put your own pastor and your own people on camera. Phone clips work.
  • Show the invitation to families within about a 10-minute drive of your church.
  • Text every family who plans a visit, and give their names to your greeters before Christmas Eve.

Start 3 to 4 weeks before Christmas Eve

Families settle their Christmas Eve plans in the weeks before. An invitation that runs for three or four weeks has time to reach them more than once, so start early and spread the budget across the whole run.

  1. Now to Wednesday, November 25Set your Christmas Eve service times and gather footage. Start your church's texting registration now, since approval takes 1 to 3 weeks.
  2. Thursday, November 26 to Thursday, December 3Launch your invitations on Facebook and Instagram.
  3. Saturdays, December 5, 12 and 19Send your welcome team the names of families planning to visit.
  4. Wednesday, December 23Text every family a reminder with their service time.
  5. Thursday, December 24Christmas Eve. Your greeters know who's coming.
  6. JanuaryInvite your Christmas guests back. Easter 2027 is March 28.

Starting in December? Invitations can start the day you approve them, and a shorter run still reaches families before Christmas Eve. Texting registration is the slow part, so file it first.

Invite families to one service, at one time

"Join us this Christmas" is easy to scroll past. A specific invitation gives a family a plan: the 5:00 candlelight service, with kids check-in at 4:30 by the tree. Say the service time out loud and put it on screen. End with one button that says Plan your visit.

If you have several services, name them all in one video, or make one invitation per service time.

Put your own people on camera

Raw beats polished for invitations. Thirty seconds of your pastor talking to a phone in the lobby says more than stock footage of a church that isn't yours. Your livestream already holds good moments, like worship or a laugh during the sermon.

A short shot list covers it:

  • Your pastor, 20 to 30 seconds, talking to the camera and naming the night and the service time
  • Your front doors, so families recognize the building when they pull in
  • Greeters at the door and the room during worship
  • Kids check-in, if you have it
  • Christmas details, like the tree and the candles

Film vertically for Facebook and Instagram, and keep each video under a minute.

Keep it close: about a 10-minute drive

The families who live near your church are the ones who can come back in January. A tight radius keeps your budget on them. In a big Texas metro that radius might cover your side of town. In a small town it can cover the whole community.

Meta removed ad targeting tied to religion in January 2022, so church ads reach people by location and age. For a Christmas Eve invitation, location matters most anyway.

Boost a post, or run a campaign?

Boosting a post from your church's page is the fastest way to start, and it works for a small push. Ads Manager gives you more control. You can draw a radius around your building and set firm start and end dates. You can also run several videos at once, and Meta puts more of the budget behind the one families respond to.

Either way, give the invitation the full 3 to 4 weeks and check on it every few days.

Give every family a next step

Send the button to a Christmas plan-your-visit page with your service times and a short note on kids check-in. Ask for a name and a phone number so you can save the family a seat.

Then follow up like a host. Send a welcome text when a family plans a visit and a reminder the day before. By Saturday, their names belong on your greeters' list, so someone can welcome them by name on Christmas Eve.

Register for church texting early

Phone carriers make every church register before texting guests. The registration is called A2P 10DLC, and approval takes 1 to 3 weeks. File it in November so your welcome texts can go out the day a family plans a visit.

Questions pastors ask

Is it too late to advertise our Christmas Eve service?

Usually not. A campaign does best with 3 to 4 weeks before Christmas Eve, which is Thursday, December 24, 2026. Invitations can start the day you approve them, so a December start still gives families time to see yours.

Can churches run Facebook and Instagram ads?

Yes. Churches advertise on Facebook and Instagram like any other page. Meta removed ad targeting tied to religion in 2022, so church ads reach people by location and age.

Should we spend the whole budget the week of Christmas?

No. Spread it across 3 to 4 weeks. Families plan Christmas Eve ahead, and steady invitations give them time to see yours more than once.

Do we need professional video?

No. Phone clips work, and raw beats polished for invitations. Thirty seconds of your pastor and a few moments from your livestream make a good Christmas Eve invitation.
