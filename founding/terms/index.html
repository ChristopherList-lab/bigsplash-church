Founding Church Terms

Version 2026-10-23

These terms cover the monthly plan between your church and Big Splash at the founding church rate. We kept them short and plain. Your church agrees to them when it subscribes at checkout.

In short

  • Your church pays $1,497 a month, month to month, and keeps that rate for as long as it stays on the plan.
  • Your 90-day promise: if your church isn't happy in its first 90 days, cancel by email with no cancellation fees.
  • After 90 days, either side can stop with 30 days notice by email.
  • Your church pays its campaign budget straight to Meta. We never mark it up.
  • Filming at your church is optional and quoted separately.
  • Everything we build stays yours, and we never promise a number of families.

1.The two parties

Big Splash Media LLC is a video and marketing studio at 1119 Ascot St., Georgetown, Texas 78626. Chris List runs it. In these terms, “we” and “Big Splash” mean Big Splash Media LLC.

“Your church” means the church named at checkout. The person completing checkout confirms they have the authority to sign for the church.

2.Your founding rate

Big Splash offers a founding rate to its first 5 partner churches: $1,497 a month, in place of the regular $1,997. Your church keeps this rate for as long as it stays on the plan.

3.What each month includes

  • A web page where new families plan their visit, built in your first month and kept up after that
  • Your Facebook and Instagram campaign, run for you and shown to families near your church
  • Up to 4 new versions of your invite videos, cut from footage you have or from a filming day
  • Follow-up texts to each family who plans a visit, from a number in your church's name
  • The monthly family report: each family who planned a visit, and each one your welcome team greeted
  • A short call to plan the next month

We set your launch date together and confirm it by email.

4.Billing

Your church pays its first month at checkout, by card or bank account. After that, Stripe charges $1,497 on the same date each month to the card or bank account used at checkout and emails a receipt. A bank payment takes a few business days to clear. To change the card or bank account, email Chris.

5.Your campaign budget

The campaign budget is what Meta charges to show your invitation to families near your church. It is separate from our fees. Your church pays it from its own Meta ad account, straight to Meta, and we never mark it up. Budgets start at $500 a month, and your church decides the amount.

We run the campaign inside your church's ad account with the access you give us. The payment method on that account belongs to your church. Big Splash never holds or controls your church's ad account funds, and we never spend past the budget you approve.

6.Filming at your church (optional)

The monthly plan does not include a filming day. Have your own videos? We can use them. If your church wants a filming day, we send a quote first and bill it separately once you approve it. Travel inside Texas is included. Outside Texas, your church covers travel at cost, plus a $350 travel day.

7.Your church's part

  • Access to your Facebook page, Instagram account and Meta ad account, plus a link to your plan-your-visit page from your website
  • Your logos, service times and kids ministry details
  • Your approval of each video and message before it goes out. Nothing that speaks to what your church believes runs without it.
  • A welcome team that marks each family's arrival on Sunday with our check-in link
  • If you add a filming day: a date, a staff point person for the day, permission to film on your property and permission from each person who appears on camera. We bring a simple release form, and a parent signs for any child.

Your church confirms it has the right to share the logos, footage and other material it gives us. Slow replies can push back the launch date.

8.Everything we build stays yours

Your church owns the videos, the plan-your-visit page, the follow-up messages and your list of families. Post the videos anywhere you like, during the plan and after it ends. Our own tools and templates stay with Big Splash.

Family information belongs to your church. We use it to run your campaign and send your report, and we never sell it.

We show your church's work as a sample only with your written permission. An email from you counts.

9.No promised numbers

We never promise a set number of families. Your count depends on your town and your campaign budget, and it needs your welcome team to mark each arrival. The monthly family report shows the real number, so your leadership can judge the campaign for themselves.

Meta sets its own rules and prices. If Meta rejects or pauses an invitation, we fix it and send it back for review.

10.Cancelling

  • Your 90-day promise: in your church's first 90 days, cancel by email any time. The plan stops at the end of the month you've already paid for, with no notice period and no cancellation fees.
  • After 90 days: either side can stop the plan with 30 days notice by email.
  • If one side breaks these terms: the other side tells them in writing. If the problem stays unfixed after 15 days, the other side can end the plan right away.

Stripe stops charging when the plan ends. Your church keeps everything we built up to that day.

11.If a payment fails

Stripe retries a failed payment and emails you, and we reach out too. If a monthly fee stays unpaid 15 days after we tell you, we may pause the plan until your account is current. During a pause we turn off the campaign in your Meta account so your budget stops spending.

12.Limits of liability

Each side's total responsibility under these terms is capped at the fees your church paid Big Splash in the 3 months before the claim. Neither side is responsible for indirect losses, such as lost income. Big Splash is not responsible for decisions Meta makes about your account or your invitations.

13.Texas law

Texas law governs these terms. If a disagreement comes up, we start with a phone call and try to work it out together. If that fails, the courts of Williamson County, Texas hear it.

14.The whole agreement

These terms, plus the price your church subscribed at, make up the whole agreement. A change counts once both sides agree to it in writing, and email works. Your church stays on version 2026-10-23 unless both sides agree to a new one.
